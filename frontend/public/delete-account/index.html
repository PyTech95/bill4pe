Bill4Pe Account Deletion

Bill4Pe users can request permanent deletion of their account and associated personal data.

Delete your account from the Bill4Pe app

  1. Sign in to your Bill4Pe account.
  2. Open your account/profile settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion request.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have access to the Bill4Pe app, send an account deletion request from your registered email address.

Request Account Deletion

Email: bill4pe@gmail.com

Important: Deleting your account will permanently remove account information and associated application data including expenses, invoices and wallet history, subject to any information that must be retained for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention or regulatory purposes.

Verification

For security purposes, we may verify that the deletion request originates from the registered account owner before processing it.
